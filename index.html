Our Story

We build the builders of tomorrow.

NEXUS HACK was born from a simple belief: the best products are built under pressure, by passionate people, with nothing to lose. Since 2018, we've hosted 7 editions, 3,200+ participants, and watched 14 teams go on to raise a combined $47M in funding.

We obsess over the details — the quality of mentors, the API credits you get, the food at 3am. Our job is to remove every obstacle between you and your best work.

Got Questions?

FAQ

Everything you need to know before and during the hackathon. Can't find your answer? Reach out on Discord.

Anyone 18 or older can participate — students, professionals, designers, developers, and entrepreneurs are all welcome. Solo hackers and teams of up to 4 are both accepted. We especially encourage first-time hackers and underrepresented groups to apply.
Yes, participation is completely free. We cover all meals, snacks, and coffee for the entire 48 hours. Travel and accommodation are not covered, but we have a list of nearby partner hotels with discounted rates.
All code must be written during the hackathon window. You may plan, sketch wireframes, form teams, read documentation, and set up boilerplate beforehand. Implementation starts at kickoff on Day 1 at 7:00 PM.
Bring your laptop (charger!), any hardware you plan to use, and comfortable clothes. We provide everything else: power strips, monitors to borrow, adapters, food, sleeping pods, and shower facilities.
Participants get access to sponsor APIs (Claude, GPT-4, Stripe, GitHub Copilot), $10K in AWS credits, hardware borrowing pool including VR headsets, fast gigabit WiFi, workshops, and 1:1 mentor sessions bookable via the dashboard.
Projects are evaluated on Innovation (30%), Technical Complexity (25%), Design & UX (25%), and Real-world Impact (20%). Each judge scores independently on a 1-10 scale. Scores are averaged. Top 10 teams present at demo day for final judging.
Yes, absolutely. You can use any open-source library, framework, or UI kit. You may also use existing design systems and starter templates. The core functionality and creative direction must be your own.
Submissions open 24 hours before the deadline via your participant dashboard. You'll submit a project title, description, GitHub repo URL, demo video (2-3 min), and optional files. Deadline is Day 3 (Mar 16) at 7:00 PM sharp.
NEXUS HACK 2025 is in-person only. We believe the magic happens in the physical space — the late-night conversations, spontaneous collaboration, and shared exhaustion. We do plan a hybrid edition in 2026.
We have a dedicated team formation event on Day 1 and a teammate-matching system in your dashboard. Many of our best projects were built by teams that formed at the event. Coming solo is totally fine!
