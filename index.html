Community Standards

CODE OF CONDUCT

FORGE HACK is committed to creating a safe, welcoming, and inclusive environment for everyone.

🤝
Be Respectful
Treat all participants, mentors, judges, and organizers with respect. Harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary behavior of any kind will result in immediate removal.
🏗️
Build Honestly
All code must be original and written during the event. Using open-source libraries is fine with attribution. Pre-built commercial products may not be submitted as hackathon projects.
♿
Inclusive by Design
We actively work to make this event accessible to participants with disabilities. Quiet rooms, dietary accommodations, and accessibility aids are available — contact us in advance.
🔒
Responsible Tech
Projects that could be used to harm, deceive, or exploit people will be disqualified. We encourage building technology that is safe, ethical, and beneficial to society.
